Defect report samples

Reproducible issues with clear user and product impact

BUG-EDR-042Critical

Alert queue does not refresh after threat detection

Expected

A new alert appears in the queue shortly after a detection event, without a manual refresh.

Observed

The alert remains hidden until the analyst reloads the page, delaying live threat triage.

BUG-EDR-058High

Incident type filter includes unrelated results

Expected

Filtering by “Spyware” shows only incidents classified with that type.

Observed

Network incidents also appear, despite the stored incident type being correct in the database.
